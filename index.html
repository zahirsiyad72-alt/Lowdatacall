How the data budget works
  • You set a budget. Auto quality picks the best video that lets it last your whole meeting.
  • You get a warning about 10 minutes before the limit. You can extend or switch to audio only.
  • At the limit, video pauses and audio continues until you extend.
  • Direct and encrypted. Media goes straight between devices, or via an encrypted relay on strict networks.
